# Project stewardship and the transition from IATF

Approved by mugiwarafx, founder and current director, as the operating
policy for LibreAccessibility projects.

## Repository custody and maintainers

LibreAccessibility holds the project repositories in the `accesslibre`
GitHub organization. Maintainers may receive repository administration
access; organizational ownership remains with LibreAccessibility.

Each accepted project has a named maintainer who leads technical work,
reviews contributions and keeps essential project knowledge in the
repository. Code, documentation, issues and release history provide the
public record of the work.

## Organization-level decisions

The board oversees project acceptance and decisions to reassign or
archive a project. Maintainers direct the technical work within each
project's agreed scope.

For now, mugiwarafx coordinates proposals, maintainer handovers and
project status changes while the two board seats are open. Public
project decisions record who made them, the date and the reasons in the
project repository or this repository's `decisions/` folder.

Board appointments and voting follow the organization's governing
documents.

## Handover, pauses and archival

When a maintainer steps away, LibreAccessibility seeks a replacement.
A handover records repository access, outstanding work, the release
process and essential technical notes. Repository ownership and history
stay with the organization.

Development may pause while a replacement is sought. If continuing the
work is no longer realistic, the project may be archived, with its
status and reason made public. Archived public repositories keep their
code and history available.

## Former IATF chair and community election

From 11 November 2026, LibreAccessibility replaces the separate IATF
community chair role and its annual community election with the project
stewardship model above. The chair election previously scheduled for
December 2026 under that former model will not be held.

Earlier chair rules, election schedules and records remain historical
material. They describe the former IATF model and do not determine
appointments to LibreAccessibility's board.

The [historical chair folder](../chair/) preserves that context.
