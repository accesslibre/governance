# Proposal review and project commitments

Approved by mugiwarafx, founder and current director. This policy
complements the [project stewardship policy](2026-003-project-stewardship-and-iatf-transition.md).

## Review

Sending a proposal does not guarantee acceptance. Review begins when
LibreAccessibility receives it. The current website form prepares an
email draft, which the proposer must send.

mugiwarafx currently coordinates review. The coordinator considers the
accessibility need, whether the work can be developed as open source,
and whether a maintainer has the capacity to take it on. The board
oversees acceptance under the stewardship policy.

The coordinator may ask the proposer for more information or discuss a
different scope before a decision is made.

## Outcomes and communication

The coordinator communicates the outcome privately using the contact
details provided, explaining the reason and any next step:

- **Accepted:** an agreed scope and named maintainer are identified
  before work starts.
- **Deferred:** the proposal needs to wait, for example because no
  maintainer is available. The reason and what would allow another
  review are explained.
- **Declined:** LibreAccessibility explains why it cannot take on the
  proposal.
- **Revised scope:** an alternative is discussed with the proposer
  before acceptance of the revised project.

## Scope and maintenance

Before work starts, the organization and maintainer agree the scope and
who will maintain the project. These responsibilities are documented in
the project's repository.

Acceptance alone does not promise a delivery date or ongoing support.
Either requires an explicit agreement, recorded in writing with the
responsible maintainer and the organization. Any ongoing support
agreement states what it covers and its duration.

Changes in maintainer availability, pauses and archival follow the
stewardship policy.

## Records

Public project decisions record the outcome, date, decision-maker and
reasons in the project repository or `governance/decisions/`. Any agreed
delivery or support commitment is documented with the project scope.

Publication must respect the privacy commitment made on the proposal
form. Private correspondence stays separate from public decision
records.
