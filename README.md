# governance

Public operating policies and decision records for LibreAccessibility,
including earlier work as the International Accessibility Task Force
(IATF).

## Current policies

- [Proposal privacy, publication permission and retention](decisions/2026-006-proposal-privacy-and-retention.md).
- [Direct proposal intake and delivery receipts](decisions/2026-005-direct-proposal-intake.md).
- [Proposal review, outcomes and project commitments](decisions/2026-004-proposal-review-and-commitments.md).
- [Project stewardship, maintainer handovers and the transition from IATF](decisions/2026-003-project-stewardship-and-iatf-transition.md).
- [Repository names](decisions/2026-002-libreaccessibility-repository-names.md).

The former IATF community chair and annual election model is historical.
Current project responsibilities follow the stewardship policy above.

## What lives here

| Folder | Contents |
| ------ | -------- |
| `chair/` | Historical IATF chair rules and records. |
| `decisions/` | Operating policies and project decisions, with earlier IATF records. |
| `costs/` | Operating cost records, by year. |
| `requests/` | Published, redacted summaries of accessibility requests. |

Each folder has its own `README.md` describing the format used for entries
in that area.

## What does **not** live here

- **Raw intake from the website form.** Those go to the private
  `intake` repository. Records here are summaries that have been
  filtered, redacted, and approved for publication.
- **Project source code.** Each accepted project lives in its own
  descriptively named repository.
- **Live discussion.** Conversations happen on Discord and in repo
  issues. Decisions that affect a project or governance are summarized
  into this repository when they are made.

## Editorial policy

- Plain English source. Translations may be added later.
- Policy decisions are append-mostly. Personal-data corrections and
  removal follow the privacy policy; preserving history does not override
  those responsibilities.
- Personal contact details, private addresses, and identifying
  information about individual users of assistive technologies are
  removed before anything is published here.
- Proposal summaries also require the requester's affirmative approval
  of the exact text before publication.
- Records are excluded from search engine indexing where technically
  practical.

The current [privacy and retention policy](decisions/2026-006-proposal-privacy-and-retention.md)
supersedes older IATF publication and retention wording, including the
historical website's "Public request records" section.

## Contributing

Entries are added by a director or a maintainer with the appropriate
repository access. If you have a correction or want to flag something
missing, open an issue or email <contact@libreaccessibility.org>.

See the org-wide
[CONTRIBUTING](https://github.com/accesslibre/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
and [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/accesslibre/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
