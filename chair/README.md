# Historical IATF chair records

The separate IATF community chair role and annual community election
were retired on 11 November 2026. The election previously scheduled for
December 2026 under that model will not be held. Current responsibilities
are recorded in the
[project stewardship and transition decision](../decisions/2026-003-project-stewardship-and-iatf-transition.md).

The material below preserves the former model and record format.

This folder holds public records about the IATF chair role:

- Term announcements and end-of-term notes.
- Election calls and results.
- Step-down or extraordinary-election notices, when they happen.

The former chair role, election rules and continuity policy are described
at <https://iatf.cc/governance/> under "Chair". That description is
superseded by the transition decision above.

## File naming

`YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md`, ordered by the date the record applies to.

## Minimal entry shape

```markdown
---
date: 2026-12-15
type: election-result | term | step-down | extraordinary
chair: <github-handle>
---

Short description of what was decided and why. Keep it factual.
```

Under the former model, the chair was IATF's public point of contact.
The current contact is <contact@libreaccessibility.org>.
